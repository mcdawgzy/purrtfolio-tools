## Day 4 — the coin-flip rule (image: 3-lesson.png)

> Someone shows you a 3:1 strategy that wins 30% of the time. Good?
>
> Check it against 1 / (1 + 3) = 25%. That's what a coin flip wins at 3:1.
>
> At 2:1 it's 33%. At 5:1, 17%.
>
> Any win rate near that line isn't an edge. It's geometry.
